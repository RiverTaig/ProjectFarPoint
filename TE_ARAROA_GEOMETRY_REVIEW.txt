Design decisions:
- Snap start/end to nearest segment within 100 m, interpolating within edges.
- Reject endpoints over tolerance with readable distance information.
- Reject selections spanning disconnected chains; do not bridge or credit gaps.
- Submit separate activities on either side of a transfer.
- Retain raw immutable GeoJSON and derive connected chains with stable IDs.
- Store trail registry and versioned GeoJSON in Supabase; about 1.1 MB is
  a reasonable initial table-based asset, loaded once per processing request.
- Preserve attribution/license and publish via SQL/import scripts initially.
- Database SQL and import scripts belong to step 3 and are not implemented yet.
